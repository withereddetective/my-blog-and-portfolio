# How to Play Arrow Game

1. Select **Start Game** and listen to the direction played.
2. Repeat the sequence using the arrow keys or the on-screen **up**, **down**, **left**, and **right** buttons.
3. Each correct sequence adds one new direction. Keep remembering and repeating the full sequence to build your score.
4. The round ends when you press the wrong direction. Your best score is saved in this browser.
5. To enter the leaderboard, provide a name and submit your high score when it meets the qualifying score. A picture is optional.

The on-screen direction buttons light up while their sounds play. Wait until the full sequence has finished before entering your answer.
